## Coverage and nonlinear display

S is lighter overall while showing separated bands, contrary to a simple “more contrast everywhere” explanation. However **less coverage is itself a confound**: erasing negative parts can make any positive bands easier to separate. Do not equate reduced darkness with truer waves.

The manifest's CPU reference of existing point guards, at rest seed0.5/ambient0, maps field0 to size3.2/opacity0.215, field−0.5 to the size floor0.5/opacity0.080625, and field−0.8 to size0.5/opacity0. These are declared CPU guard examples, not measured GPU values. They explain why signed mathematical cancellation and visible point darkness are not linearly related: troughs can become almost absent dots rather than visible depressions. Changed source displacement is also part of the field-model comparison; this is not a tone-only ablation.

## Conclusion and implementation boundary

Confidence is **high** that this sampled signed profile produces clearer band/gap separation than the positive coverage patch; **medium** that it is a useful phase principle for the intended surface; **low** that the current point result establishes water relief or realistic overlap. Its strongest competing explanation is selective negative-lobe invisibility, not a newly convincing fluid surface. A continuous signed-height presentation directly addresses that display limitation without requiring objects or a stateful solver.

Proceed to the reusable surface implementation and generous opening/no-Resume playground using the agreed factory boundary. Preserve input response, distinct phase progression and readable overlap when selecting the continuous presentation. Treat lighting, gradients, mesh sampling and any changed coefficients as declared production choices. Inspect both positive and negative regions rather than judging a dramatic highlight alone. Keep the pale-paper identity and real Resume functions; verify the actual intro, controls, reduced motion and fallback in the integrated browser flow.

Do not tune Stage 2 wavelength/gain after the fact or begin another broad visual survey. These findings justify a concrete delivery direction, not a finished-surface claim. No objects, new dither or reference-provenance claim is supported.
